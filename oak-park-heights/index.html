Getting here from Oak Park Heights

From the Highway 36 stores

If you're running errands along 36 (the Walmart on Norell Avenue, Kowalski's on Neal Avenue), get on Highway 36 heading east toward the river. Take the Highway 95 exit (Stillwater / Bayport) and turn right to go south. Stay on 95 about a mile and a half. It becomes 3rd Street in Bayport, and we're on your left, next to the American Legion and the post office.

About 7 minutes, 3.7 miles from the Walmart lot. About 6 minutes, 3.2 miles from the middle of town.

Coming back over the St. Croix Crossing

Highway 95 is the first exit on the Minnesota side after the St. Croix Crossing bridge. Take it, head south, and you're here in a couple of minutes. Wisconsin shoppers, please read the guide for Wisconsin shoppers before you come: what you buy here stays in Minnesota.

Drive time and distance: OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), checked October 22, 2026, without traffic. Allow extra time when traffic is heavy.

One exit, no detour

Oak Park Heights sits right between Stillwater and Bayport, so for most of town we're a straight shot down 95. If you work in Bayport or commute past the Andersen campus, we're already on your way. Andersen employees get 10% off on Tuesdays with their Andersen ID.

We're one locally owned store. I built most of it myself, and you can see the before-and-after on the build page.

Where to park

Use the paved lot behind the building (turn onto 1st Ave N, then take a left down the alley) or street parking on 3rd Street out front. If those are full, there's a public lot at City Hall across the street. The building has entrances at both the front and the back, so come in whichever way is closer.

Deli flower by the gram or by the ounce

Looking for a dispensary near Oak Park Heights where an ounce won't break the budget? Deli flower is our budget-friendly way to buy flower. It's weighed to order at the counter on certified scales, right in front of you, anywhere from 1 gram up to two ounces, the Minnesota limit for one purchase.

Mix strains within a tier and the combined quantity sets your price, so a mixed ounce is still $210 or $225. The more you buy, the less each gram costs. In the $11 tier, a single gram is $11, 3.5 g works out to $10.00 a gram, 14 g to $8.57 a gram, and a full ounce to $7.50 a gram. The $12 tier steps down the same way, to $8.04 a gram at the ounce. From the Highway 36 stores it's about 7 minutes, so it fits on the way home.

Before your first visit

  • Have your ID out. We card everyone, every time, 21 and up only. A license from any state is fine, and so are passports, passport cards, Tribal IDs, and Canadian IDs. Expired IDs can't be accepted.
  • Cash or debit only. Debit runs through a cashless ATM with a $3 fee, and there's a regular ATM on site.
  • Join the loyalty program with your phone number. It's 3% back in points, and members see the deals.
  • Not sure what you want? Ask at the counter, or browse on the in-store kiosk at your own pace.

Local rules: Oak Park Heights regulates cannabis businesses in City Code Chapter 1413. Not verified: we didn't find a separate Oak Park Heights rule on public use. In Bayport, City Code Sec. 26-77 (Ordinance 891, adopted August 7, 2023) bans smoking cannabis or hemp products in public places, and Sec. 26-28 bans open containers of cannabis or hemp drinks on streets, sidewalks, and other public places. Oak Park Heights questions

How far is My Dealer from Oak Park Heights?

About 3 to 4 miles. From the center of Oak Park Heights it's about 6 minutes without traffic, and about 7 minutes from the Walmart on Norell Avenue. Highway 36 east to the Highway 95 exit, then south.

Can I preorder and grab it on my way home?

Yes. Order at mydealermn.com/bayport, then pick up the same day or the next day. You pay at pickup, and we check ID at the counter.

Is there parking?

Yes. There's a paved lot behind the building (turn onto 1st Ave N, then left down the alley), street parking on 3rd Street out front, and the public lot at Bayport City Hall across the street. The building has entrances at both the front and the back, so come in either way.

What are your hours?

Noon to 8, every day. If that changes, we'll update the site.

Can I use cannabis in the car or in the parking lot?

No. Minnesota law doesn't allow cannabis use in a motor vehicle, parked or moving, and our lot is no exception. Take it home. Minn. Stat. 342.09
