No. There is no on-site consumption, and Minnesota law doesn't allow cannabis use in a motor vehicle, parked or moving. Bayport bans smoking or vaping cannabis in public places, including the store's lot.

FAQ

Minnesota dispensary FAQs

All FAQsMinnesota Cannabis Laws
What are your hours?

Noon to 8, every day. Longer hours are on the way, and I'll update this page when they change.

Where do I park?

The building has entrances at both the front and the back, so come in whichever way is easier. There's a paved lot behind the building: turn onto 1st Ave N, then take a left down the alley. There's also street parking out front on 3rd Street (Highway 95), and a public lot at City Hall across the street.

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. Everyone has to be 21 or older with a valid ID. We check ID every visit, no exceptions.

What ID do you accept?
  • A driver's license or state ID from any U.S. state
  • A Canadian driver's license or ID
  • A Tribal ID
  • A U.S. passport or passport card
  • A Minnesota instructional permit, if you're 21 or older
  • A passport, if you're a foreign national

We can't take expired IDs, altered IDs, or a foreign driver's license or national ID card (that's state law, not us being picky). The photo has to match you.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Use the live menu at mydealermn.com/bayport to place a preorder for pickup the same day or the next day. You pay when you pick up, and bring your ID. If an order isn't picked up by the end of the next day, it goes back into inventory.

Do you take credit cards?

No. We take cash, or your debit card through our cashless ATM. We also have a regular ATM on site. The cashless ATM has a $3 fee, the same idea as a regular ATM. It runs in $10 steps, so your withdrawal rounds up to the nearest $10 and we hand you the difference back in cash.

How much cannabis can I buy at once?

Minnesota caps a single purchase at:

  • 2 ounces of flower (pre-rolls count by weight)
  • 8 grams of concentrate (vapes count here)
  • 800 mg of THC in edibles, shared across cannabis edibles, hemp edibles, and hemp THC drinks

Each edible serving can have up to 10 mg THC and each package up to 200 mg. Drinks top out at 10 mg per serving and two servings per container.

For carrying: up to 2 ounces of flower in public, up to 2 pounds of flower at home, and up to 8 grams of concentrate and 800 mg of edible THC.

Can I use it in the parking lot or my car?

No. Minnesota law doesn't allow anyone to use cannabis in a motor vehicle, parked or moving, driver or passenger (Minn. Stat. 342.09, subd. 1(b)(2)). That includes our lot. For the ride home, keep products in the trunk or rear cargo area unless they're still sealed in the original package. Use it at home or on private property where it's allowed.

Do you sell medical cannabis?

No. We're an adult-use store and sell to adults 21 and older only. For how Minnesota's Medical Cannabis Program works, see the Office of Cannabis Management's Division of Medical Cannabis at mn.gov/ocm/medical-cannabis. We can't diagnose or certify anyone, and this isn't medical advice.
