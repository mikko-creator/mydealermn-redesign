First, the rule: buy in Minnesota, use it in Minnesota

I'd rather lose a sale than have you get in trouble, so this goes first. What you buy at My Dealer is legal in Minnesota only. Don't carry cannabis across state lines, and that includes the I-94 bridge back to Hudson. Wisconsin treats THC (outside of legal hemp) as a controlled substance, and Hudson has its own possession ordinance on top of state law. Every package we sell says it: "This product may be unlawful outside the state of Minnesota."

So plan where you'll use it on this side of the river before you come: a private residence, or private property where the owner is fine with it. Not in the car, not in a park, not in our lot. Our guide for Wisconsin shoppers has the statutes, the Hudson and St. Croix County ordinances, and the rest of the detail.

Local rules in Hudson and North Hudson

City of Hudson Code Chapter 118 (amended in full August 19, 2024) bans possessing or using marijuana, with a forfeiture of $50 to $500 plus costs. For more than 25 grams, or after a prior conviction, the city can cite you only if the St. Croix County District Attorney declines to charge. The same chapter makes it illegal for anyone under 21 to possess hemp-derived THC products such as delta-8 or THCA. Hudson Code ch. 118

St. Croix County Chapter 58 also bans marijuana possession (up to 25 grams, first offense), with a forfeiture of $125 to $500. St. Croix County ch. 58 (PDF) The Village of North Hudson's code (Sec. 70-12) also prohibits marijuana possession or use; we haven't verified its fine. North Hudson code State law applies on top of all of these.

In Bayport, City Code Sec. 26-77 (Ordinance 891, adopted August 7, 2023) bans smoking cannabis or hemp products in public places, and Sec. 26-28 bans open containers of cannabis or hemp drinks on streets, sidewalks, and other public places. Bayport Code ch. 26

Licensed cannabis vs. a hemp shop

There are shops on the Wisconsin side that call themselves dispensaries. They sell hemp-derived products, which is a different, lower-potency category with different rules. My Dealer is licensed by the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management as a cannabis retailer (license DIS-L24-000241), and our flower is grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators and tracked by the state. We carry some hemp-derived products too, and those are labeled as hemp. You can look our license up on OCM's list.

Hemp THC on December 11: what changes, and what doesn't

Heads up for Wisconsin shoppers looking for cannabis near Hudson. Under current federal law, starting December 11, 2026, a finished hemp product with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per package (the can, bottle, or bag it's sold in) no longer meets the federal definition of hemp. So do hemp products made with lab-converted cannabinoids. That covers most hemp THC gummies, vapes, and drinks, including delta-8. This is a federal change, not a new Wisconsin law. Wisconsin's hemp law hasn't changed, and two Wisconsin bills to regulate hemp THC products, AB 747 and SB 682, did not pass.

Minnesota has its own hemp law. The Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management says the federal change doesn't change how Minnesota defines lower-potency hemp edibles and drinks, or whether they're legal under Minnesota law. OCM also notes that many hemp products that comply with Minnesota law, such as packages with more than 0.4 mg of THC, won't meet the new federal definition, which could affect hemp businesses' banking, taxes, and interstate shipping.

Licensed Minnesota cannabis was never hemp. It's what most people mean by real weed: grown as cannabis by licensed Minnesota cultivators, tested, and tracked by the state. It's legal under Minnesota law only, and it's still illegal under federal law and in Wisconsin. Come visit Bayport, about 10 minutes from Hudson. Everything you buy here, cannabis or hemp, is for use in Minnesota only.

Where Wisconsin cannabis law stands

As of October 22, 2026, Wisconsin has no adult-use or medical cannabis program. In the 2025-2026 session, lawmakers introduced a medical cannabis bill, a legalization bill, and several bills to regulate hemp THC products. None passed; all failed on March 23, 2026, under the session schedule, after the last regular floor period ended in mid-March. Hudson questions

Can I buy at My Dealer with a Wisconsin driver's license?

Yes. Any adult 21 or older with a valid, unexpired photo ID can buy here, within Minnesota's purchase limits. Wisconsin licenses work the same as Minnesota ones.

Can I bring it back to Hudson?

No. Cannabis isn't legal in Wisconsin, and taking it across the river is illegal. The City of Hudson's own ordinance (Chapter 118) carries a $50 to $500 forfeiture, and state law applies on top of that. Buy in Minnesota, use it in Minnesota. Details and sources are on our Wisconsin shoppers page.

Is there a licensed dispensary in Hudson?

No. Wisconsin has no adult-use or medical cannabis program, so there are no state-licensed cannabis dispensaries there. Shops in Wisconsin that use the word dispensary sell hemp or CBD products.

Where can I buy THC near Hudson after December 11?

If a "dispensary near me" search in Hudson turns up hemp shops, that's because Wisconsin still has no licensed cannabis stores. Under current federal law, starting December 11, 2026, hemp products with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per package no longer meet the federal definition of hemp. For real cannabis, My Dealer is a Minnesota-licensed cannabis retailer in Bayport, about 10 minutes from downtown Hudson, for adults 21+ with valid ID. Licensed cannabis is legal under Minnesota law only, and everything we sell is for use in Minnesota only.

How long is the drive from Hudson?

About 10 minutes and 5.7 miles from downtown Hudson without traffic: I-94 west over the river, exit 258 for Highway 95, then north about 4 miles to Bayport.

Where can I legally use it in Minnesota?

At a private residence, including the yard, or on private property that isn't open to the public, as long as the owner hasn't said no. A business or event licensed for on-site use works too. Not in a vehicle, not in public places, not in our parking lot, and no smoking or vaping inside apartment buildings, balconies included. If you're visiting, sort that out before you shop.
